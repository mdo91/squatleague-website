How daily and weekly squat leaderboards work

Understand which workouts count toward Game Center rankings.

By Damas Technologies · October 21, 2026

Get ready

Connect Game Center and explicitly join ranked challenges. Use Friends or Everyone to choose the rankings you view; the Friends filter does not make a submitted score private.

In the app

Complete a ranked challenge to add its genuine reps to your cumulative daily and weekly totals. Ordinary solo workouts and live online duels do not add leaderboard scores.

Keep in mind

Daily periods reset at 00:00 UTC; weekly periods reset Monday at 00:00 UTC. Totals on separate devices are not combined. Rankings are recreational results, not independently verified athletic records.

Squat League offers general fitness counting and visual cues. Tracking depends on framing, lighting and body visibility; it is not a medical measurement or a substitute for individual coaching.
