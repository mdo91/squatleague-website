How to track squat workouts and achievements

Find saved sessions, follow workout-day streaks and collect 17 badges.

By Damas Technologies · October 21, 2026

Get ready

Finish a positive-rep session to save its summary on your device. Open History and choose a day in the calendar to review reps, duration and movement statistics.

In the app

Open Achievements to see lifetime squat milestones, workout-day streaks and eligible podium badges. Open a badge to read its requirements; earned badges can be shared through the iOS share sheet.

Keep in mind

History does not sync through iCloud and devices do not combine totals. Game Center achievement sync is separate from detailed history. Clearing local data removes local records; Apple may retain already reported achievements.

Squat League offers general fitness counting and visual cues. Tracking depends on framing, lighting and body visibility; it is not a medical measurement or a substitute for individual coaching.
