How to start a squat challenge with friends

Use Game Center and Squat League Pro for live, head-to-head squat duels.

By Damas Technologies · October 21, 2026

Get ready

Both players need Squat League, Game Center access, an internet connection and active Pro access to start or accept a duel. Give each phone a clear full-body view.

In the app

Open Online and choose Challenge a Friend. Select a 1-, 3- or 5-round preset and invite your friend through Game Center. Follow the readiness prompts before the match begins.

Keep in mind

Each work round lasts 60 seconds, with 30-second rests between rounds. Accepted reps determine the result; ties draw. Your opponent receives your profile and live score, not your camera footage.

Squat League offers general fitness counting and visual cues. Tracking depends on framing, lighting and body visibility; it is not a medical measurement or a substitute for individual coaching.
