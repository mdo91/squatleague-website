Privacy policy

How Squat League for iPhone and iPad and this website handle information.

Updated: 2026-10-21

Camera and local history

Camera frames and body landmarks are processed in memory on your device and are not recorded or uploaded. Workout summaries, preferences, progress and pending reports are stored locally; workout history does not use iCloud synchronization.

Optional Game Center services

Apple operates Game Center profiles, friends, rankings, achievements and matchmaking. Joining ranked challenges shares your profile and challenge totals. Friends filters do not hide scores from Everyone. Achievement sync sends identifiers and percentage progress to Apple; it can be disabled per account.

Online duels and badge sharing

Duels exchange profiles, rules, readiness, timing information, rep events, accepted scores and results with an opponent. Camera footage and form measurements stay local. Sharing an earned badge creates an image on-device and opens the iOS share sheet; nothing is posted automatically.

Subscriptions

Apple processes Pro subscriptions and payment information. StoreKit verifies access and purchases on-device. We do not receive payment card details or send transaction payloads to a custom server. Deleting the app or clearing workouts does not cancel a subscription.

Website and support

This website uses GitHub Pages, whose provider may log IP addresses and ordinary request information for security. The site has no advertising, analytics scripts or tracking cookies. Information you email to support is used to respond to your request; avoid sending sensitive images or health information.

Retention and your choices

Camera permission is controlled in iOS Settings. Clear Local Data removes local workout records and progress; this cannot be undone. Game Center scores and reported achievements may remain with Apple. Separate devices do not combine totals. Manage Apple-held data and subscriptions through Apple settings.

Contact and updates

For questions, privacy requests or support, contact hi@damastechnologies.com. We do not sell personal information. This policy will be updated if processing changes; its date identifies the current version. Apple services remain subject to Apple’s own privacy policies.
