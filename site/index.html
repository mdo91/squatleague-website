A few useful answers

Before your first rep.

What can I use for free?

Solo workouts, history, achievements and rankings are free. Live online duels require Squat League Pro.

Do I need an account?

Solo training and local history work without an app account. Game Center is required for ranked challenges and online duels.

How do live duels work?

A round lasts 60 seconds. Friend presets offer 1, 3 or 5 rounds, with 30-second rests between rounds. Most accepted reps wins; equal scores draw.

Does the app record my workouts?

It does not record or upload camera footage. Rep counts and workout summaries are saved locally. Optional Game Center features share profile and score information.

What happens if the camera loses me?

Counting pauses while tracking is unavailable. In a duel, the shared match timer keeps running.

Where can I see Pro prices?

Check the app for current weekly/monthly prices and trial eligibility. Apple processes subscriptions, which renew automatically unless cancelled in Apple subscription settings.
